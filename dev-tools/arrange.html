Hand over

Pick a device size above, then click anything on the page: a clay prop, a heading, a paragraph, a form, a button. Drag it to move it, and drag the corner square to resize. Props also rotate with the dot above them.

"Select the part around it" steps out to the block that holds what you clicked, for example from a heading to the whole text column.

Undo steps back one change at a time, back to exactly where it was; Cmd+Z does the same and Shift+Cmd+Z redoes. Each page and size keeps its own undo history until you close the tab.

"Moved at this size" lists everything you've changed here. Click a name to find it, press its Reset to put it back where the site has it, or reset the lot at once. Resets can be undone too.

Keys: arrows nudge (Shift for 10px), [ and ] rotate, - and = resize, Esc deselects.

Each size saves on its own: phone (up to 640), tablet (641 to 900), laptop (901 to 1440) and wide. The two phone sizes share one set.
